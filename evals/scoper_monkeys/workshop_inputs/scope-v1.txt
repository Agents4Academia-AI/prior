AI / LLM agents and agentic systems whose core contribution performs a scientific
research task, or rigorously evaluates the reliability or safety of such systems.

IN SCOPE: autonomous AI scientists and end-to-end research agents; agent methods
for experimentation; research-idea or hypothesis generation; automated scientific
reviewing, citation or claim verification, experimental reproduction; structured
scientific claim or contribution extraction used to assess or organise research
findings; and direct evaluations of agentic research systems.

OUT OF SCOPE: education and student assessment; generic LLM capability or agent
orchestration not tied to a research task; systematic-review search/screening
automation (a separate corpus); domain-science papers that merely use an LLM;
general software or databases; and reviews, surveys, perspectives, ethics or
agenda pieces without a concrete primary system or empirical evaluation.

BOUNDARY RULE: for autonomous laboratory or domain-science work, include only
when the agent method or evaluation is itself a core contribution. Do not include
a paper solely because an automated system was used to produce a scientific result.
